# Visual target — provisional constitution

**Status: provisional until complete-environment candidates are visually inspected.** It
is not a command to force an industrial facility, bunker, or prior story. It gives
rejection criteria so later acquisition does not produce a pile of incompatible props.

## Target

A believable **coastal utility/signal outpost** with an accessible exterior approach and
a compact enclosed building. The mood is late-evening wet weather: cool blue-grey ambient
light outside, controlled warmer practical light in key interior work areas, and one
intentionally readable navigation landmark per zone. This is a scale-conscious small
location, not a generic horror bunker and not a giant open world.

## Material / asset rules

- Architecture: weathered painted steel, sealed concrete, glass, cable, simple functional
  work surfaces and maintenance signage. Materials need believable roughness/normal detail
  and consistent texture density.
- Exterior: restrained rain-darkened masonry/metal, puddle reflection only where it aids
  readability, silhouette background with no fake distant world interaction.
- Interior: enough negative space for player movement, door clearance and sightlines;
  props in purposeful clusters (workbench, storage, control station), not random scatter.
- Reject cartoon, flat-shaded, toy-like, oversaturated, untextured, unrelated low-poly,
  visibly mismatched, or provenance-unverified source material. **Kenney is prohibited.**

## Camera / UI / lighting

- First-person camera at believable human height; moderate FOV; no constant head bob,
  violent recoil or lens effects that hide collision/aiming errors.
- UI is a restrained objective/prompt/ammo-health layer with no copied reference layout.
- Lighting must have physical-looking sources and a hierarchy: route / interaction /
  atmosphere. Darkness cannot substitute for missing composition.

## Visual acceptance for a future room

A room needs a full browser screenshot review at player height and must score at least 90%
on composition, scale, materials, light hierarchy, prop logic, navigational clarity,
negative space, performance and defect absence. An uninspected room is *unrated*.
