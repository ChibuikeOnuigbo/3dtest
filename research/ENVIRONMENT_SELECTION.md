# Environment and art-direction lock — *Pale Beacon*

## Selected environment

A compact, original **Kori Bay coastal civil-safety signal outpost** during a bright overcast dawn squall. It has a paved jetty/arrival platform, a small weather-sealed keeper’s
building, relay gallery, generator bay, radio workshop and lantern gallery. The exterior
water/horizon is a compositional boundary, not a large traversable sea.

This explicitly rejects a bunker, generic factory, warzone, abandoned mega-complex,
open-world coast and random-kitbash approach. The building is a functional public-safety
site with human-scale circulation, electrical/communications equipment and maintenance
routes that explain each door, stair, cable and prop group.

## Locked visual grammar

| Element | Direction | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Space | 4 m modular bays; 2.1 m door clearance; low generator room vs taller lantern space. | Player capsule/door/rail clearance must be authored before décor. |
| Materials | Salted painted steel, sealed concrete, dark glass, galvanized rails, rubber cable, restrained wood work surface. | Three-to-five material families only until browser review; no unlicensed texture drops. |
| Light | Bright cool exterior fill and skylight; warm work practicals; amber service lights; final rotating beacon. | Every key light has a source and navigational/gameplay role. |
| Colour | Blue-grey weather, oxidized warm metal, sparse amber/green/red state indicators. | Indicator colours have state meaning; no neon noise. |
| Landmarks | Dark tower/lens visible at arrival; cable trunk in relay gallery; green generator status; final beam. | Repeatedly reinforce current objective and return path. |
| Props | Desk/radio together; isolator/cable/maintenance case together; rails/antenna on roof. | No unmotivated crates/barrels/pipes or intersections. |

## Content allocation

| Zone | Approx. playable function | Planned visual/technical budget |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival Jetty (R1) | Tutorial / stakes / exterior composition | Bright horizon, restrained rain particles only if profiled, one distant beacon. |
| Keeper’s Hall (R2) | Tool acquisition / instructions | Two practical lights, work desk/cabinet cluster, clear door views. |
| Signal Court (R3) | Skylit hub / sentry state / wing orientation | Central signal spindle, low bridge rails, windows into both wings and one moving sentry; no dense repeated clutter. |
| Generator Bay (R4) | Cause/effect objective | One hero generator/isolator, audio/light shift after power. |
| Workshop + Lantern (R5) | Signal routing / final payoff | Radio desk, exterior view, one beam effect only after ending action. |

## Asset decision

Original modular geometry is the approved base for blockout. No external model/texture is
approved at this point. The pending asset ledger may only enhance this authored set after
its intake gate passes; it cannot change room scale, collision or story function.
