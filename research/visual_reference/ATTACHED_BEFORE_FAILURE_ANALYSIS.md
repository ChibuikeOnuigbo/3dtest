# Attached screenshot review — failure reference

**Source:** player-provided capture set (`map_000` through `map_019` listed in the
conversation attachment metadata). These images are treated as visual review input only;
they are not copied into game assets.

## What is visible

The captures show a third-person black cat moving among stylized floating grass islands,
large pale stone portal rings, pink blossom trees, white/bright-cyan sky, scattered coin
routes and distant floating fragments. Several views include a large blank cyan void and
long falls; another shows the character camera clipping close to the cat.

## Hostile visual critique

| Finding | Severity | Evidence visible in the supplied captures | Required response for this project |
|---|---|---|---|
| Void reads as the world boundary | CRITICAL | Multiple fall frames contain almost entirely cyan gradient with a small falling character. | Build ground/roof foundations, continuous city/hill/water backdrop, haze and clear safe fall/reset volumes. |
| Generic floating-island obstacle-course composition | CRITICAL | Repeated detached rock platforms dominate the horizon. | Use a connected rooftop/overpass district with trusses, bridges, service paths and believable supports. |
| Visual style is inconsistent with fast FPS target action | MAJOR | Cat character, coins, blossom trees and fantasy portal rings create a different game identity. | First-person only; no mascot, coins, fantasy rings or copied portal theme. |
| Repeated pale stone and flat green ground | MAJOR | Most reachable surfaces use the same few pale/green treatments. | Material families need roofing, concrete, painted steel, glass, rubber and route paint at proper scale. |
| Traversal route has weak architectural explanation | MAJOR | Many slabs/rocks sit detached from a broader built environment. | Every playable bridge/platform is supported by a building, truss, crane, stair or visible structural member. |
| Camera can collide with character | CRITICAL | One image is almost entirely obstructed by the player model. | There is no third-person avatar in this game; first-person camera is collision-separated. |
| Scenery lacks a coherent distant place | MAJOR | Isolated islands/fragments sit in uniform sky. | Background needs a cohesive industrial skyline, distant bridges, roof fields, cranes, water/hills, and atmospheric depth. |
| Repeated bright portal circles compete with navigation | MEDIUM | Many coloured ring structures appear at once. | Limit visual accents; reserve a single clear endpoint/transition language and use route colour sparingly. |

## Positive lessons to retain abstractly

- The images provide long-distance destination visibility and a readable elevated-route
  premise. The new game needs equivalent vistas, but grounded in a believable urban
  structure.
- Traversal should give the player a broad view of the next region, a recovery option,
  and strong vertical rhythm.

## Result

**FAIL as a visual target for Rivet Run.** The attached capture set establishes what the
new world must actively avoid: empty cyan falls, detached obstacle islands, uncontrolled
accent structures, third-person camera obstruction, repeated surfaces and no place-like
exterior context.
