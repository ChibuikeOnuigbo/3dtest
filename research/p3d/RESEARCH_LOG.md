# P3D review

**Source checked:** <https://p3d.in/> on 2026-09-05.

P3D presents itself as a WebGL/AR model sharing/viewing platform with public
models and creator pages. The platform's existence and preview functionality do
not establish a blanket asset licence. No P3D asset, model, texture or preview
has been downloaded, embedded or shipped. A future candidate needs the specific
creator, source URL, exact terms, material/UV/scale/poly inspection, browser
performance plan and in-world use review before it can enter the asset manifest.
