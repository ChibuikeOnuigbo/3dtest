# Story Bible — The Last Signal

## Setting
Ravenscar Weather Relay, a compact coastal public-service station. A storm has cut the relay that sends a tide-surge warning to three shoreline communities. It is late evening; the sea is not visible from the playable rooms, but wind, rain and the antenna beacon make its pressure present.

## Player
A lone on-call maintenance technician who receives an automatic fault alert. No named character model, dialogue tree or cinematic is required.

## Central problem
The automatic transmitter has gone offline during an approaching surge. Its thermal fuse was removed after a false-overheat alert, and a breaker trip sealed the transmitter gallery. The player has one shift log and the physical building to understand what to restore.

## Opening
The player enters a dim airlock. An inactive status console identifies an emergency relay fault and points to the duty office log. Wind and a distant relay tick establish the place.

## First objective
Read the duty office's maintenance sheet: **replace the marked thermal fuse, then reset the shore breaker**.

## First discovery
The required fuse is in a labelled red case in the service passage—not a random key. Its empty socket is visible behind the power-room panel.

## Escalation
Fitting the fuse changes the panel from fault red to armed amber. Resetting the breaker turns on room lighting, a low electrical hum and unlocks the gallery security door. The player sees a new cyan glow at the far transmitter console.

## Midpoint
The transmitter is alive but holds the last safe frequency. A tuning note on the duty desk establishes that the storm-warning channel is 3–1–4. The interaction is contextual: the same station log explains the order of its three dials.

## Secondary discovery
The active console identifies the saved message as the crew's final automated weather bulletin. The player is preserving a routine civic system, not defeating an antagonist.

## Final objective
Set 3–1–4, arm the broadcast and press TRANSMIT.

## Climax
The transmission tone clears the storm noise; receiver lamps ripple across the room; the external antenna beacon stabilizes from intermittent amber to steady pale cyan.

## Ending
A clean status line reads `COASTAL WARNING ACKNOWLEDGED`. The game remains in the room with the now-lit equipment, offers a short ending card and a restart button. Completion is unambiguous.
