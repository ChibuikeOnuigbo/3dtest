# Feasibility-first game concept selection

The supplied-video evidence is blocked, so none of these concepts claims to imitate a
video. Scores use independent project constraints, confirmed source-study lessons and the
asset intake status as of 2026-09-04. Asset availability means *ability to author a
coherent small location without unapproved media*, not clearance of a third-party pack.

Scoring: 0–10 each; overall weight = coherence 15%, implementation 20%, pacing 10%,

## Why *Pale Beacon* wins

It has one comprehensible life-or-death purpose, a naturally compact loop between five
distinct spaces, and no requirement for a city, vehicles, crowds, huge enemy roster,
procedural ocean or multiple maps. A believable shelter/communications building can be
independently authored from modular architecture. The singular exterior landmark—the
unlit beacon—gives every objective a visible consequence. The selected direction is a
coastal civil-safety outpost, **not** a bunker, factory or generic military base.

## Scope guardrails

- 20–30 minutes first run; one save-free self-contained mission.
- Five required playable spaces plus only small connecting thresholds; no empty corridors
  counted as rooms.
- One sidearm-like non-lethal emergency pulse tool, one finite drone archetype and no
  multiplayer, crafting, loot, dialogue tree or fetch-chain.
- Every obstacle changes navigation, knowledge, threat, or the beacon state. A key/card
  is allowed only when it represents a visible operational condition and creates a
  meaningful route decision.
- If a coherent approved asset/material route fails, retain the original modular art plan
  and reduce decoration rather than mixing random replacements.
