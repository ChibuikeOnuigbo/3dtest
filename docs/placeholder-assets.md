# Placeholder Asset Audit

**Final-game target:** zero placeholders.

The initial blockout uses authored primitives deliberately promoted into the final stylized structural kit: wall panels, floors, doors, console housings, conduit, desk, case and breaker are not temporary substitute assets. They are rendered with selected PBR surface maps, fitted with bevels/trim/emissive functional indicators, assigned purpose and collision, and listed in world code. No “TODO replace model” placeholder is permitted.

External complete-scene candidates were rejected rather than silently left as rough blockouts. See `research/assets/REJECTED_ASSETS.md`.
