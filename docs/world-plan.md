# World Plan — The Last Signal

## Scale and route
Five rooms cover approximately 30m × 15m. The player moves along a clear eastward service route, then north into a larger transmitter gallery. Rooms use real-world door scale (2.1m), low service ceilings (3–4.2m), 1.3m+ circulation paths and purpose-built collision boundaries.

## Room approvals planned

| Room | Primary focal point | Navigation anchor | Gameplay/story job | Lighting concept | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Airlock | Fault console | red fault pulse | arrival and first interaction | cool storm fill + red console | warm office leak to east |
| Duty office | desk/work order | amber desk lamp | understand repair and frequency | warm local pool | cable tray east |
| Service passage | red fuse case | stripe/conduit run | collect contextual replacement part | intermittent amber fixture | red power fault glow |
| Power room | breaker cabinet | warning stripe / red fault lamps | fit and reset physical power path | red before, warm after | maglocked north door |
| Transmitter gallery | three-dial console | tall antenna and cyan receiver array | solve tuning and send warning | cyan after state progression | ending in place |

## Composition rules
- The route is intentionally non-symmetrical: room width, light color and major prop changes identify where the player is.
- Props are grouped by room function, never scattered randomly.
- Structural panels remain visible; no room is packed to its walls.
- Exterior weather is seen only through a controlled antenna window, avoiding an asset-heavy open world.
